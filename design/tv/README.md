# Presentation video for a venue screen

`presentation.html` is the animated source (1920 × 1080). Open it with `#play` at the end of the address to watch it.
Scene lengths can be passed in the address (`?d=11.77,12.51,…`) so the pictures follow a narration.

`narration.json` holds the French narration, one entry per scene. The voice of the delivered video was generated
with Piper and the voice `fr_FR-siwis-medium` (SIWIS French speech database, University of Edinburgh, licence CC BY 4.0).
In the spoken text the product name is written « Eup zone » and « e-mail » is written « imèle » so the voice says them the French way.

The people and the QR code shown are drawings; the QR code leads nowhere.
